ArtVista Exhibition 2025

About the Exhibition

ArtVista Exhibition 2025 showcases contemporary art from emerging local artists. The exhibition features over 50 stunning artworks including paintings, sculptures, and digital art. Our mission is to promote local talent and provide a platform for artists to connect with art enthusiasts. The exhibition runs from March 15 to April 30, 2025, at the City Arts Center.



The exhibition space spans over 5,000 square feet across three beautifully designed galleries. Gallery One focuses on traditional paintings and portraiture, Gallery Two showcases three-dimensional sculptures and installations, while Gallery Three is dedicated to digital and mixed media artworks.

Our Mission

To celebrate local artistic talent by providing an accessible platform for emerging artists to showcase their work. We foster creativity, encourage artistic dialogue, and build a vibrant arts community.

Our Vision

To become the Northern Province's premier destination for contemporary art, recognized as a catalyst for cultural enrichment and artistic innovation. We aim to establish ArtVista as an annual tradition that celebrates artistic diversity.
